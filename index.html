About Dragon Ball

Discover the epic saga that has captivated millions worldwide

The Legendary Journey

Dragon Ball is a Japanese media franchise created by Akira Toriyama in 1984. The initial manga, written and illustrated by Toriyama, was serialized in Weekly Shōnen Jump from 1984 to 1995.

The series follows the adventures of protagonist Son Goku from his childhood through adulthood as he trains in martial arts and explores the world in search of the seven orbs known as the Dragon Balls.

Dragon Ball has become one of the highest-grossing media franchises of all time, with the manga's 42 collected volumes having over 260 million copies sold worldwide.
